# Comparison specifications in the demo inventory

The 23 additional inventory records (IDs 10–32) now include engine, horsepower,
transmission and drivetrain. Prices, stock counts and inventory years remain demo
values; these records do not certify that a particular model was produced in the
inventory year. Specifications use the named variant below, rather than substituting
a newer or more powerful trim.

Power is stored as mechanical `hp` to match the comparison parser. Where the source
uses metric PS/CV, convert with `hp = PS × 0.98632` and round to a whole number.
Hybrid figures are combined system output. The Taycan uses its normal 777 hp
output, rather than temporary Launch Control/overboost output.
